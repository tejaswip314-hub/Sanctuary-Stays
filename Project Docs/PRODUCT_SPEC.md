## 1. Product Vision & Positioning

Sanctuary is an ultra-curated hospitality collective that brings architectural integrity, acoustic serenity, and turnkey white-glove service to vacation stays across India and the world.

### Core Differentiators vs Standard Vacation Rentals
1. **Top 1.8% Curation Rate:** Open platforms list 90%+ of applicant properties resulting in listing clutter, wide-angle deception, and inconsistent quality. Sanctuary only accepts homes that pass an in-person jury evaluation.
2. **Turnkey "Zero-Chore" Departure Guarantee:** No stripping sheets, washing dishes, or taking out garbage bins. Housekeeping operations guilds handle 100% of the turnaround.
3. **In-Person Acoustic Audits (< 22 dB):** Calibrated sound level mapping during day and night hours ensures master suites are whisper-quiet, shielded from road, traffic, and barking dog noise.
4. **Artisan Indian Textiles & Culinary Standards:** Pure mulberry silk, 600-thread organic Indian cotton bedding, Blue Tokai and Araku micro-lot coffee, and ceremonial single-estate Darjeeling teas.
5. **Royal Concierge Liaison:** Dedicated 24/7 personal liaison assigning in-villa Royal Maharaj chefs, private backwater solar Shikara cruises, and helicopter transfers.

## 3. Collections & Regional Taxonomy

### Curated Collections
1. **Palace & Heritage Havens:** Fortified sandstone bastions, restored havelis, and royal estates.
2. **Himalayan Sanctuaries:** High-altitude passive solar retreats, rammed earth pavilions, and slate cantilevers.
3. **Coastal & River Pavilions:** Tropical Indo-Portuguese villas, riverside glass houses, and backwater teak sanctums.
4. **Forest & Plantation Retreats:** Restored British tea planter bungalows, rainforest treehouse canopies, and coffee estate retreats.
5. **Metropolitan Residences:** Art Deco sea-facing addresses, Lutyens bungalow estates, and Chettinad/Bengal courtyard mansions in India’s great cities.
6. **World Sanctuaries:** Signature international retreats — Kyoto machiya, Balinese bamboo pavilions, Tuscan poderi, Moroccan riads, Cycladic cave villas, Cape Dutch farms, and Alpine chalets.
